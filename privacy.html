Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

About this application

Personal Backup Rclone is a privately operated configuration of Rclone used by its owner to maintain encrypted backups in the owner's Google Drive account.

Google Drive data access

The application requests Google Drive access so the owner can create, list, verify and restore backup data. It is not offered as a public service.

Data storage

Backup content is encrypted locally before it is uploaded to Google Drive. OAuth credentials are stored locally on the owner's computer. Google Drive stores the encrypted backup files.

Data sharing

This application does not sell personal data or intentionally share Google Drive data with advertisers or third parties. Google processes data according to its applicable policies.

Data retention and deletion

The owner controls backup retention and deletion. Google Drive access can be revoked through the owner's Google Account security settings.

Contact

For privacy enquiries, contact the application owner through the support information displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
